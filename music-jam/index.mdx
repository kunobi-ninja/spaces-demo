{/*
  The pattern lives in the music-jam-pattern store: the tempo, the key, and one row per track with
  its instrument, owner, volume and 16 steps. data/pattern.json is its readable copy, shown when the
  store cannot open. locales/<lang>.json translates the starting track names (data/pattern.start.json)
  by id, and the page's words: a track still named as it started shows translated. In steps, "." is a rest; drums use "x" (and "X" for a loud hit); bass, chords
  and lead use 1 to 9, the note's place in the key's scale. These are only the page's own words.
*/}
