# Why we pay our coffee farmers 30% above fair-trade

Our beans come from two cooperatives, in Guji and in Huila. We pay 30% above the fair-trade minimum and buy the same lots every year.

That costs us about 0.20 euros per cup. We think it is the best 0.20 we spend: farmers can plan, invest in drying beds, and pick only ripe cherries. You taste that in the cup.
