# How we work

## Shifts

| Shift | Hours | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bake | 04:00 to 11:00 | Ben, plus the second baker once hired |
| Morning counter | 07:00 to 14:00 | Chloe and one counter person |
| Afternoon counter | 13:00 to 19:00 | Two counter people |
| Office | Flexible | Ana and Dev |

Swap a shift with someone who has the same role, then tell Chloe. She keeps the rota.

## Who decides what

- **Ana** decides anything that costs more than €500, and anything about the lease or the landlord.
- **Ben** decides recipes, ovens and kitchen equipment.
- **Chloe** decides the counter, the rota and what we post.
- **Dev** decides suppliers' payment terms and the till setup.
- Anything else: whoever owns the task decides, and writes it in [Decisions](decisions.md) if it matters next month.

## Working with our AIs

Each of us uses our own AI: Ana and Dev use Claude, Ben uses ChatGPT, Chloe uses Gemini. They all work in this shared folder.

- **Shared work lives here.** The task list, this wiki and the menu are the source of truth. If your AI drafts something the team needs, it goes here.
- **Private notes stay private.** Your own chats, drafts and personal notes stay with you. Only put things here you are happy for the whole team, and their AIs, to read.
- **Ask, then check.** An AI can update a task, draft a supplier email or cost a recipe. A person checks it before anything is sent or bought.
- **Say who did it.** When your AI changes a page, the history shows it was your AI working for you.

## Weekly check-in

Mondays at 15:00, 20 minutes. We look at [This week](../index.mdx), anything Waiting on the [board](../projects.mdx), and new [decisions](decisions.md).
