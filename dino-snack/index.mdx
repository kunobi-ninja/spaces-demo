# Dino Snack for {KID}

<DinoSnack kid={KID} />

Move the dinosaur with your finger, the mouse or the **arrow keys**. Catch ten pieces of fruit in
its mouth and keep out from under the rocks: the red spot on the path shows where one is about
to land. Three rocks and the dinosaur needs a nap. **Space** starts a round and **M** turns the
sound off and on.

Change `KID` at the top of this page to make the game for someone else.
