## Felipe

“[ChatGPT dots - Felipe](https://superspl.at/scene/ca6a4c9b)” by abstrakt,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**Changes made:** we took the second published level of detail
(1,746,690 Gaussians), converted it from SOG to SPZ version 3 with one
spherical-harmonics band using SplatTransform, and split it into three files
(`assets/felipe-1.spz` to `felipe-3.spz`) so each stays under the 16 MiB asset
limit. The page orients, lights and animates it at playback. The original is
at <https://superspl.at/scene/ca6a4c9b>.
